Tips for parents and caregivers

Discover our recommendations to best support your child in playful learning

Adapted duration

Limit sessions to 15–20 minutes to avoid fatigue and maintain optimal engagement.

Supportive guidance

Stay present to encourage and guide the child, especially during first uses of the games.

Positive reinforcement

Praise every effort and progress, even small, to strengthen motivation and self-confidence.

Regular repetition

Repetition consolidates learning. Replay the same games several times a week for better results.

Respect the pace

Each child progresses at their own pace. Be patient and adapt difficulty to their abilities.

Calm environment

Create a calm play space without distractions to support focus and learning.

Communication

Encourage the child to express emotions and preferences during play for better support.

Professional collaboration

Feel free to share progress with therapists for coordinated, effective care.
