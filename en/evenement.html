“Together for an inclusive society where every person counts”

Associations TSA Advocacy Sénégal

The Plateforme Autisme au Sénégal (P.A.S) is a federating body that brings together associations engaged in autism. It aims to promote an inclusive, equitable society that respects the rights of autistic people, through advocacy, awareness-raising, capacity building and improved access to educational, health and social services.

Key Data
