In accordance with P.A.S statutes, the platform is administered by a Board of Directors of seven (7) members elected by the General Assembly and an Executive Board of five (5) members, the governing body responsible for executing GA decisions, ensuring the federation runs well, and steering the permanent secretariat and thematic committees.
