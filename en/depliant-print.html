1/160 autistic children worldwide (WHO)
0.8% autistic children in Senegal
CONTEXT

In Senegal, 0.8% of young children are affected by autism. Facing a severe shortage of medical, paramedical, and educational resources, coordinating stakeholders is an absolute priority.

OUR MISSION

Coordinate the actions of member associations to improve care for children living with Autism Spectrum Disorders (ASD).

GENERAL OBJECTIVE

Unite associations working on autism to promote an inclusive, equitable society that respects the rights of autistic people.
