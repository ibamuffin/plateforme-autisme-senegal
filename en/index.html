About us

Our Vision

“Together for a more inclusive society where everyone counts”

Our Mission

Unite associations working on autism in Senegal to improve care for children with ASD through advocacy, training, resource sharing, and public awareness.

Overall Goal

Unite the actions of associations engaged in autism to promote an inclusive, equitable society that respects the rights of autistic people, through advocacy, awareness-raising, capacity building, and improved access to education, health, and social services.

Context and Rationale

Autism is a condition that is increasingly visible worldwide. According to WHO, 1 in 100 children has Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), and 15 million people are affected by autism in Africa. In Senegal, estimates are around 0.8% of the population according to CHU Fann.

Situation in Senegal

In Senegal, the limited statistics available indicate that “0.8% of young children are affected by autism” according to the child psychiatry centre at Fann Hospital.

Current challenges

  • Severe shortage of medical resources
  • Lack of specialized allied health professionals
  • Insufficient adapted educational resources
  • Limited specialized infrastructure
